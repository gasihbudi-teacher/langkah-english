# Langkah English V13-C5 · TKA-09 and TKA-10 release

Prepared: 31 October 2026. Baseline: the user-accepted corrected combined V13-C4 source, including the separately supplied homepage redesign, TKA-01 through TKA-08, all legacy learning pathways, the three-format quiz engine, browser-local progress and backup features, and the three original UTBK mocks.

## What is included

| Module | Focus | Scaffold steps | Explained cards | Embedded practice | Topic quiz |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| TKA-09 · Five TKA text genres | Descriptive, recount, narrative, procedure and analytical exposition; purpose, structure, audience and language clues | 14 | 10 | 3 | 12 items |
| TKA-10 · Question formats and independent practice | Single-answer, multiple-answer and category tasks; instruction words, evidence, distractors, completeness and self-review | 9 | 10 | 3 | 12 items |

Both lessons use Indonesian explanations, English-first worked examples, one guided 200–300-word model with a full Indonesian translation, ten distinct bilingual examples, three embedded practices, one checkpoint, one reflection, an independent self-check, and vocabulary at the end.

Each untimed topic quiz contains two original unseen passages: an A2 editorial target of 200–300 words and a B1 editorial target of 250–350 words. Each quiz has four single-answer, four multiple-answer and four category questions. Items include exact evidence quotations, Indonesian explanations and translated prompts/options/statements. Single-answer keys are balanced across all four positions.

## Pedagogical emphasis

TKA-09 teaches genre recognition through communicative purpose, structure, audience, paragraph function and language features. The five genres are descriptive, recount, narrative, procedure and analytical exposition. Students are warned not to classify from topic or one signal word alone; a text may include secondary functions while retaining one dominant purpose.

TKA-10 develops independent response habits across the three formats supported by the Langkah English practice engine. Students parse instruction words, locate evidence, test every option, complete every category row and review their work. Exact-set multiple-answer scoring and all-rows-correct category scoring are described only as local platform practice rules, not official TKA scoring policy.

## Verification boundaries

A2/B1 labels are editorial word-length and difficulty targets, not external CEFR certification. Materials are original, AI-assisted and unofficial TKA-style practice, not official examination papers or score predictors. Automated checks do not establish teacher approval, learner outcomes, WCAG certification, real-device accessibility or live deployment. A bilingual teacher review and learner pilot remain recommended.

## Installation

Use `langkah-english-v13c5-combined-update-from-v13c4.zip` on top of the user-accepted corrected combined V13-C4 repository, preserving relative paths and `.git`. Do not use this patch on older C1/C2/C3 sources. Do not extract the complete-site ZIP over an existing Git repository; it is for separate preview or restoration only. Run `python3 tools/validate.py` and `for f in tests/*.mjs; do node "$f" || exit 1; done` before committing. Optionally run `python3 tests/v13c5-browser-smoke.py` where Playwright and Chromium are installed.
