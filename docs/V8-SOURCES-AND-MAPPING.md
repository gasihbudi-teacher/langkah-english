# Langkah English V8 — Curriculum sources and mapping (reviewed 25 October 2026)

## Status and limitations
This is an **unofficial, original, AI-assisted** English learning collection. Lessons and question sets are **not reproductions** of textbooks, official exam papers, or schools' tests. The mapping is **thematic and competency-based**, not an assertion that all Indonesian schools must teach a chapter in a particular grade or term. Each school develops teaching objectives from the applicable *Capaian Pembelajaran*; the government textbooks are useful examples of possible sequencing.

A practising English teacher should review grammatical accuracy, level of complexity, Indonesian explanations and distractor quality before classroom assignment. These modules emphasise **reading–viewing** and introductory language use. They are **not** sufficient evidence of full curriculum coverage, which also requires listening–speaking and writing–presenting.

## Source hierarchy, relevance, and eligibility

**Primary policy and curriculum (highest weight)**

1. **Pusat Kurikulum dan Pembelajaran / BSKAP (2025), *Panduan Mata Pelajaran Bahasa Inggris Fase B–F*** — authoritative government subject guide for Fase D, E and F outcomes and genre/text-based instructional approaches. Eligible because it is issued by the Indonesian ministry's curriculum authority and directly defines intended competencies. https://repositori.kemendikdasmen.go.id/33628/ (accessible PDF: https://repositori.kemendikdasmen.go.id/33628/1/3.%20Final%20Panduan%20Mata%20Pelajaran%20Bahasa%20Inggris_12_09_2025_Revisi%203.pdf)
2. **BSKAP 046/H/KR/2025**, *Capaian Pembelajaran*, as published on the Ministry's BPMP domain — formal basis for CP phase mapping. Eligibility: government decision, not a commentary or commercial syllabus. https://bpmpbabel.kemendikdasmen.go.id/PPID/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/KepKaBSKAP-046_2025-ttg-CP.pdf
3. **BSKAP (2025), *Panduan Pembelajaran dan Asesmen*** — guidance for formative checking, feedback and sequencing. Eligibility: government education assessment publication. https://repositori.kemendikdasmen.go.id/33290/

6. **Pusat Perbukuan, *English for Change*, SMA/MA Kelas XI** — online identities, environment, healthy living, environmental figures and personal money management. **Official entrance examination framework (do not conflate with TKA)**

8. **SNPMB, *Framework UTBK-SNBT 2026*** — defines English Literacy as 20 questions / 20 minutes, using science/technology, social humanities and personal contexts and requiring finding, understanding, evaluating and reflecting on information. Eligibility: the examination organiser is the primary source. https://snpmb.id/fr/
9. **SNPMB, *UTBK-SNBT Information*** — confirms the examination components. Eligibility: official organiser. https://www.snpmb.id/utbk-snbt/informasi-umum
10. **Pusmendik, *TKA Bahasa Inggris SMA*** — useful contrast for school reading genres and competencies; **TKA is a different assessment from UTBK-SNBT**. Eligibility: government assessment authority; this site does *not* market these V8 sets as TKA mocks. https://pusmendik.kemdikbud.go.id/tka/tka/view/mata-pelajaran-wajib/sma/bahasa-inggris

**Supplementary context only**

Established commercial tutoring providers and university admissions pages can be consulted for alternative examples or confirmation, but do not supersede government specifications. No proprietary passages or item banks have been copied into V8.

## How V8 modules are taught

Each module uses a three-step Indonesian scaffold → five-word vocabulary support → 3 easy sentence examples → no-grade checkpoint → sentence breakdown → 2 contrast examples → reflection → 2 optional longer examples → original English model passage → recap → two short English-only questions with expandable Indonesian glosses → a linked four-question English reading quiz with evidence-supported Indonesian explanations. The new material preserves separate collapsible **Materi/Latihan** groups and the independent Search page.

Practice Mode allows optional Indonesian question/choice translations and immediate feedback. **Simulation Mode intentionally hides these aids until results**, reducing the risk of presenting translation-assisted work as timed unaided reading performance. The 3 original 20-question, 20-minute V7 UTBK mocks are unchanged. The three *V8 UTBK labs* are four-question topic practices, **not** full UTBK simulations.

## Known limitations and next steps

- Current Grade 9–12 expansion predominantly covers reading; more listening, speaking, writing and presentation assessments are needed.
- Grade 7–8 prerequisite competencies are linked through the existing Grammar Academy, but not all Phase D competencies have dedicated grade-specific modules.
- All V8 assessment passages and explanations need independent subject-matter review and real-device QA. The numerical quiz score is a local practice result, **not** an official UTBK/TKA score, scale, rubric or predictor.
- To add a lesson: edit `data/lessons/ID.json`, its matching `data/quizzes/ID-practice.json`, and `data/catalog.json`. Maintain English questions/choices, bilingual gloss alignment, exact evidence quotations and unique IDs. Run the validation workflow before publishing.
