# Langkah English V8 — Curriculum expansion

## What's added
- **20 grade-specific school lessons**, five each for Kelas 9, 10, 11 and 12, drawn from the thematic and competence areas highlighted in the official government English textbook sequence.
- **20 new matching school reading quizzes**, four original multiple-choice questions each, with Indonesian glosses for questions and every option.
- **Three UTBK reading-skill lessons and three matching short quizzes**, covering scientific evidence, social-science proportions and evidence-based inferences (12 new quiz questions). They are not full UTBK mocks.
- For every new lesson: three-stage scaffold, 7 worked example sentences with Indonesian meaning and distinct explanation, vocabulary, a short guided checkpoint, a sentence breakdown, a comparison pause, an original English passage, a summary and **two embedded English-first exercises**.
- Curriculum metadata for phase, broad skill, topic/genre and explicit non-official mapping caveats.
- Connections to existing foundational lessons through `prerequisites` and `relatedLessons`.
- **Mode clarity:** optional Indonesian glosses and instant checking in untimed Practice Mode; translations and explanations are hidden during timed Simulation Mode and shown afterward in results.

## Counts
- V7: **45 resources** = 30 lessons + 15 quizzes; 234 graded questions and embedded practice questions, plus 30 non-graded checkpoints.
- V8: **91 resources** = 53 lessons + 38 quizzes; 372 graded questions and embedded practice questions, plus 53 non-graded checkpoints.
- Added by V8: 23 lessons, 23 quizzes, 23 original quiz passages, 92 quiz questions, 46 embedded practice questions, 23 checkpoints, 161 worked-example cards.
- All 234 V7 questions remain unchanged, including answers and answer positions, so completed practice results remain comparable.

## Scope, academic integrity and warnings
All new passages and practice questions are original, AI-assisted and unofficial. The school-grade mapping is thematic, not a fixed national chapter-by-chapter timetable. The score is for learning feedback, not official certification or predicted university entry. Content and assessment questions should be reviewed by qualified bilingual teachers, and browser/mobile flows need real deployment testing.

See `docs/V8-SOURCES-AND-MAPPING.md` for the full evidence hierarchy, sources and grade-by-grade mapping.
