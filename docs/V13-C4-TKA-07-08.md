# Langkah English V13-C4 · TKA-07 and TKA-08 release

Prepared: 31 October 2026. Baseline: **the user-accepted corrected combined V13-C3 source**, which includes the redesigned homepage, TKA-01 through TKA-06, the three original UTBK mocks, and all previous assessment and progress features.

## What is included

| Module | Focus | Scaffold steps | Explained cards | Embedded practice | Topic quiz |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| TKA-07 · Facts, opinions and sufficient evidence | Verifiable statements, opinions, claims, source quality, relevance, sampling, sufficiency, counterevidence and proportional conclusions | 12 | 10 | 3 | 12 items |
| TKA-08 · Narrative appreciation | Narrator, setting, conflict, character development, theme, mood, point of view, imagery and figurative language | 10 | 10 | 3 | 12 items |

Both lessons use Indonesian explanations, English-first examples, one complete guided model (200–300 words), ten distinct bilingual worked examples, three ungraded embedded practices, one checkpoint, one reflection, an independent self-check, and vocabulary at the end.

Each untimed topic quiz uses two unseen full passages: one A2 editorial target (200–300 words) and one B1 editorial target (250–350 words). Each quiz has four single-answer, four multiple-answer and four category questions. Every item includes an exact passage evidence quotation, an Indonesian explanation and translated prompts/options/statements. Single-answer keys are balanced across the four option positions.

## Pedagogical emphasis

TKA-07 teaches students not to confuse a verifiable statement with a proven conclusion. Learners assess whether evidence is relevant to the claim, how the source and sample were obtained, whether the amount and type of evidence are sufficient, and what alternative explanations or limitations remain. The lesson distinguishes correlation from causation and encourages conclusions that do not exceed the data.

TKA-08 treats appreciation as evidence-based interpretation rather than unsupported personal reaction. Learners examine how setting and conflict shape a story, how actions and dialogue reveal character, how a character changes, and how theme, mood, point of view and figurative language affect meaning. Symbolic interpretations must be supported by the narrative context.

A bilingual teacher review and a small student pilot remain recommended. The A2/B1 labels are editorial word-length and difficulty targets, not external CEFR certification. These are original AI-assisted, unofficial practice materials, not official TKA papers or score predictors.

## Installation

Use `langkah-english-v13c4-combined-update-from-v13c3.zip` on top of the **user-accepted corrected combined V13-C3 repository**, preserving relative paths and `.git`. The patch adds TKA-07/TKA-08 and their quizzes while retaining the integrated homepage redesign and all prior modules. Do not apply this patch directly to an older C1 or C2 source. Do not extract `langkah-english-v13c4-combined-complete-site.zip` over an existing Git repository; use the complete-site ZIP only for a separate preview/restoration copy.

Run `python3 tools/validate.py` and `for f in tests/*.mjs; do node "$f" || exit 1; done` before committing. Optionally run `python3 tests/v13c4-browser-smoke.py` where Playwright and Chromium are installed. Test both new lessons and quizzes on a real phone, then verify the live deployment after publishing.
